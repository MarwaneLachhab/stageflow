# StageFlow — Design mockups (illustrative concept)

All layouts are in French and follow the supplied cahier des charges.
Brand name “StageFlow” and sample applicant names/statistics are illustrative placeholders.

## Public pages
01 Accueil — landing page, calls to action, key benefits.
02 Inscription — nom, prénom, e-mail, téléphone, password and confirmation.
03 Connexion — e-mail/password and account access.

## Stagiaire role
04 Tableau de bord — current application, quick actions and status.
05 Profil — personal and academic information, edit controls.
06 Formulaire de demande — stage details, motivation, save as draft, multi-step progress.
07 Documents et soumission — file uploads and explicit final submission.
08 Suivi de la demande — status history, documents and eventual administrative decision.

## Administrateur role
09 Dashboard — five KPI metrics, analytics and recent activity.
10 Gestion des demandes — search, filters, application table, statuses.
11 Détails et décision — full applicant file, document list, acceptance/refusal including optional reason.

Status workflow: Brouillon → Envoyée → En cours d’examen → Acceptée OU Refusée.
The HTML mockups are static visual references; they do not include authentication, database or functional app logic.
